## 3. What the market number means

- `~555/hr` is one 20-minute sample (600 blocks, 185 settlements, 89 buyers). Across 62 stored snapshots
  the figure ranges 411 to 34,680 per hour (median 7,190) and mean price per settlement $0.009 to $140.
  It is not a rate you can plan on.
- Organic filter (buyer paid >=2 sellers on >=2 days): 5% of the sample, ~18/hr, ~430/day for the whole
  network. At $0.006-$0.03 per call that is ~$3-13/day network-wide.
- What the sampled calls buy (top sellers by calls): tweet-quote lookups ($0.0064), AX1 Console token
  "roast" verdict ($0.018; consumer UI with a wallet login and shareable card; 218 distinct buyers over the
  sampled days), text sentiment ($0.03), Pocket Network JSON-RPC, LLM chat ($0.036), Exa search, Zerion wallet
  data, SMS. One unlisted seller at $0.001 had 558 distinct buyers (a mass-distribution or game-style mint).
  Our address: 0 hits in the sample.
- Our prices ($0.001-$0.01 data, $0.05 MCP scan) sit at or below the $0.0061 median payment and well below
  the $0.018-$0.036 sellers that actually move volume. Price is not what limits us: both our rank-1 Bazaar
  slot (`defi yields`) and our best route yield ~$0.02/month.
