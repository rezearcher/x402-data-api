# Surface reorder: lead machine-import surface with the token-safety lane

**Date:** 2026-10-21
**Lane:** B (supply / discoverability)
**Lever:** surface-lead-with-measured-demand-lane

## Why

Ground-truth on-chain demand (`~/.hermes/data/x402-demand/onchain_demand_by_lane.json`,
generated by `scripts/onchain_demand_by_lane.py` scanning each competitor payTo in our
lanes) ranks lanes by **real USDC settled**:

| lane | external buyers | $ observed | competitor max $/call | our route | our $/call | edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **token-safety** | 6 | 5.158 | 0.25 | /gate/swap | 0.008 | **31.2×** |
| yields | 2 | 2.01 | 0.02 | /defi/yields | 0.001 | 20× |
| wallet | 0 | 0.00 | 0.15 | /chain/wallet | 0.003 | 50× |

Token-safety is the **#1 live-demand lane** with our biggest price edge. But the
`/.well-known/x402` `resources[]` array (the surface an agent auto-wires from) still
carried the cycle-35 ordering that LED with `/pm/markets` and buried `/gate/swap`,
`/chain/token-report`, `/chain/token-security` at position ~54. That cycle-35 reorder
was justified by the romankurnovskii *census* ("token-safety is dead, demote it") — a
conclusion cycle 40 overturned with direct on-chain settlement measurement. The surface
was never re-pointed to match the corrected evidence; this cycle does that.

## What shipped

- `src/index.ts`: moved the three token-safety routes to the TOP of `resources[]`;
  rewrote `service_overview.tagline`/`description` to lead with the safety gate and the
  measured price advantage (rivals up to $0.25/call vs our $0.008).
- `scripts/surface_demand_alignment_sensor.py` (+ cron `47 */6`): asserts the live
  surface's lead resource == the top measured-demand lane's route, so the ordering can
  no longer silently rot into a stale hand-pick. Degrades open. Pre-deploy run correctly
  flagged `aligned:false` (lead `/pm/markets` != expected `/gate/swap`).

## Not thrash

The A2A agent-card (`/.well-known/agent-card.json`) already led with `gate-swap`; this
aligns the x402 manifest + description to the same evidence-backed ordering.
